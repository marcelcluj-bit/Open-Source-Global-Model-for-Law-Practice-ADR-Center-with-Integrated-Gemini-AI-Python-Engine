GLOBAL LEGAL & ALTERNATIVE DISPUTE RESOLUTION (ADR) HUBOpen-Source Global Model for Law Practice & ADR Center with Integrated Gemini AI & Python Engine1. Executive Summary & Global VisionThis repository presents a fully scalable, open-source operational model for a Professional Legal Practice (Law Firm) & Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) Center. The model is designed for global deployment under a unified brand while leveraging native local legal professionals to maintain strict compliance with local jurisdiction and case-law jurisprudence.To lower operational friction, accelerate dispute resolution, and ensure cross-border interoperability, the model incorporates an embedded Python-based AI Engine powered by Google Gemini API.2.
